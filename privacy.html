Phoebus: privacy

Phoebus has no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting SDK and no account of its own: everything you sign in with is your own Reddit account. Nothing about your use of the app is sold, shared or sent to the app's author.

What leaves your device

  • Reddit. Reading, voting, saving, posting and chat go to reddit.com under your own credentials, exactly as the official apps do. Some features (polls, web sign-in, modmail) use a reddit.com web session you sign in to yourself.
  • Media hosts. Images, GIFs and videos are fetched from wherever they are posted: Reddit's CDNs, Imgur, Giphy, Image Chest and others.
  • Third-party keys you enter. An Imgur, Giphy or Image Chest API key, a Kagi Session Link or an AI provider key is sent only to that service, when you use the feature that needs it.
  • Your own servers, if you set them up. Notification and subreddit-watcher alerts go to the notification backend and Bark server you configure, on your own network or host.
  • Search. If you turn on search-engine lookups, the query goes to the engine you chose (Google, Kagi, or another).

What is stored

  • Your Reddit sign-in, in the app's own storage on your device.
  • Your settings and, if you enable separate settings per account, a per-account copy of the ones that support it.
  • Optional local caches of images, avatars, link previews and badge books, which you can clear from the app's settings.

Permissions

  • Network: to reach Reddit, the media hosts above, and the notification backend you configure.
  • Notifications: for alerts your backend delivers, and for subreddit watchers.
  • Photos: only when you save media to your library.
  • Location: only if you enable automatic theme switching, to work out sunset and sunrise locally. It is not sent anywhere.

Updates

A release build can check the app's AltStore source once a day to see whether a newer version exists. That check sees the source URL and your IP address, and nothing else; you can turn it off in Settings › Privacy › Automatic Update Checks.
